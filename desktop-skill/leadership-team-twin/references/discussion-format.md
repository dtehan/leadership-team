# Discussion format

Write the whole conversation, verbatim, in this layout. This is the record of the meeting.

```markdown
# Discussion: {Topic}
**Customer**: {Customer}   **Date**: {YYYY-MM-DD}   **Room**: CEO, CFO, CTO, COO, Moonshot, Contrarian{; guests: ...}
> Simulation built from public and supplied information. Not what the real executives said.

## Framing
**CEO:** {the question as put to the room, the core tension and what makes it hard}

## Guest briefing          <- only if guests are in the room
**{Guest title} (guest, no vote):** {4 to 8 paragraphs}
**CEO (facilitating):** {main claims, any disagreement between guests}

## Opening round: first instincts
**CFO:** ...
**CTO:** ...
**COO:** ...
**Moonshot:** ...
**Contrarian:** ...
**CEO (reading the room):** the tensions we need to work through: 1. ... 2. ... 3. ...

## Deliberation
### Round 1
**CEO to {Role}:** {specific challenge}
**{Role}:** {2 to 4 sentences, engaging what others actually said}
**CEO (facilitating):** {what shifted, what hardened, what is still unresolved, who speaks next}

### Round 2
...                     <- at least 4 rounds; everyone speaks at least once

## Final vote
**CFO:** SUPPORT / OPPOSE / CONDITIONAL. {one reason; the condition that would change the vote}
{one entry for each of CTO, COO, Moonshot, Contrarian}
**{Guest title} (advice, no vote):** {3 to 5 sentences}

**Tally**: SUPPORT n, CONDITIONAL n, OPPOSE n

## Decision
**CEO:** {what is decided, why, and how any strong dissent was weighed}
```

Rules: each turn is labelled with the speaker and written in their voice. Keep what each person
actually said, including disagreement and changes of mind ("I've moved on this because..."). Do
not merge turns, drop weak ones or tidy conflict away. Note a speaker's `Context gap:` where it
occurred and collect them in the memo.
