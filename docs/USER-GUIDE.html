From evidence to useful team discussions

Build the context.
Bring the right team.

A practical guide to setting up company and people profiles, assembling reusable teams, and using brainstorm, evaluate and scope for different outcomes.

Copilot CLIOne account per sessionReusable profilesExplicit team selection

Simulation, not prediction. Every generated contribution is based on public and supplied context, never a statement or prediction by the real person. Rankings, simulated decisions and plans do not constitute real organizational approval. The value is in arguments, trade-offs and dissent.

01Start and select an account

You need Git, Python 3.8+ and a signed-in GitHub Copilot CLI that supports repository skills and native custom agents. Python helpers use the standard library; no package installation is required. This repository's documented workflow supports Copilot CLI.

git clone https://dev.azure.com/danieltehan/Leadership/_git/Leadership
cd Leadership
copilot

Review the repository configuration before trusting it. Inside Copilot, use /instructions and /skills list to confirm discovery. The discussion workflows invoke the native specialist agent; you do not register or invoke an agent for each person.

New customer

Create a new customer account for Example Company with slug example-company, then build its company profile and leadership profiles.

Provide the exact legal entity, ticker if applicable, leadership roster if known, and authorized source files or links. Account creation only makes the workspace and generic skeleton; research and team creation are separate work.

Existing customer

Work on the Example Company account. Show the existing company coverage, available profiles and unresolved gaps before starting new research.

The assistant runs account-scope and retains that selection. Several teams in this account can be used in the same session. Use a fresh session for another customer.

Handle customer context deliberately. Account isolation is workflow guidance, not automatic enforcement or a security boundary. Customer folders are gitignored, but not necessarily encrypted or local-only: Copilot processes supplied context and research may use online sources. Use authorized material and never copy customer facts into shared prompts or the company template.

02Build the company knowledge base

company-research creates a progressively loadable company profile: a short company brief, area indexes and small sourced documents. It is not a single giant report. Existing evidence is checked before fetching or writing more.

Financial

Business segments, revenue and margins, costs, capital allocation, investment commitments and risks. Use filings and earnings materials; attach the reporting period to every figure.

Technical

Platforms, cloud and data decisions, security, ownership, pilots and partnerships. Distinguish what is in place, evaluating, rumoured or retired, and record evidence quality.

Strategy

Stated priorities, market position, competitors, regulation and important changes. Separate sourced facts from labelled interpretation.

Walk through the initial build

  1. Confirm the research target. Resolve the exact company and requested coverage. Supply permitted reports, links and internal background; supplied information remains labelled as supplied.
  2. Build or reuse the company evidence. The assistant inventories existing sources, creates concise topic documents and records unsupported claims as gaps instead of inventing facts.
  3. Confirm the leadership roster. Company-research calls person-profile for each selected leader, reusing stable profile IDs and known source references.
  4. Review the coverage report. Ask which areas and people are grounded, thin, pending or unresolved. A directory of names is not completed research.
  5. Check indexes. The assistant rebuilds indexes after knowledge-document changes and validates their structure. Structural checks do not certify evidence truth.
Build the company profile for Example Company and research its selected leadership profiles. Reuse existing evidence, distinguish facts from inference, and report coverage, evidence status and unresolved gaps.

What good company evidence looks like

Documents include a summary, tags, evidence date or period (as_of), source, confidence and the profile IDs that should load them. Primary evidence is preferred. An old fact does not become current simply because its source was rechecked today; retrieval and review dates belong in the research ledger.

Before assembling a team: ask for company coverage, profile evidence status and unresolved gaps. Missing evidence reduces the specificity of a simulation; it never licenses fabricated biography or figures.

03Create and reuse people profiles

person-profile is the single workflow for leaders, managers, DBAs and other professional roles. Company-research uses it for selected leadership; you can also use it independently before any team exists. Creating a profile does not assign team membership.

Show the available profiles for this account. Build a person profile for Alex Example, our database lead, using relevant technical context and attributable professional sources. Reuse an existing matching profile if one exists.
  1. Resolve the identity. Confirm name, employer, title, relevant areas and stable profile ID. Namesakes and conflicting roles require clarification, not guesswork.
  2. Reuse before creating. The same person keeps one dossier across teams. Existing IDs such as leader, cfo, cto and coo remain valid. New accounts have unused template seats that can be filled in place.
  3. Research and synthesize. Review public professional evidence and authorized supplied material. Keep sources, dates, gaps and labelled inference; unsupported sections remain unknown.
  4. Assess evidence, then validate. The assistant reports the profile's evidence status and checks its metadata and indexes. A scaffold is unfinished research, not a verified biography.

Protect identity and history. Research only public professional information and authorized supplied material. Do not infer private traits from a title. Existing real-person dossiers need requested or approved refreshes. A new person occupying an old role requires an explicit identity/history decision; never silently replace a person behind a stable ID.

04Create a named team

define-team assembles profiles into an explicit room. A team might be core leadership, extended leadership, DBAs or another working group. Creating company and people profiles does not create a team.

Define dba-team with Alex as leader, Sam and Jo as voting participants and the CTO as a non-voting advisor. It handles database operations and escalates new platform spending to the CTO.

Confirm these choices

Purpose and authority

Choose a stable team slug, display name, purpose, what the team can decide, and the escalation authority. Decisions beyond its authority become recommendations to escalate.

Room and assignments

Exactly one profile-backed leader and at least one other participant. Explicitly choose voters, non-voting advisors and optional Contrarian/Moonshot perspectives. Nothing is included automatically.

  • The leader is never included in the participant tally and weighs argument quality, not majority count.
  • Advisors are participants with vote: false; they brief first and provide advice, not votes.
  • Contrarian and Moonshot are optional synthetic perspectives, separate from real participant votes.
  • No duplicate profiles, no leader repeated as a participant, and no cross-account links, nesting or inheritance.
  • Definitions reference existing account-local profiles. Missing expertise requires a profile and an approved team change, not an ad hoc guest.
Example team definition

The account-local teams/<team-slug>/team.json is authoritative. This core-leadership example enables both optional synthetic perspectives.

{
  "name": "Core Leadership",
  "purpose": "Company-wide strategic decisions within delegated authority",
  "leader": "leader",
  "participants": [
    { "profile": "cfo", "vote": true },
    { "profile": "cto", "vote": true },
    { "profile": "coo", "vote": true }
  ],
  "facilitation": ["contrarian", "moonshot"],
  "escalates_to": null
}

Change a team without rewriting history

Update dba-team to add our security lead as a non-voting advisor. Confirm the revised room, profile limitations and escalation before running another discussion.

Use define-team to update and validate the definition. Removing someone does not delete their profile, memory, calibration or past contributions. For a one-scenario room, create another named team from the same profiles or approve an explicit membership change. Each run snapshots its definition and effective room, so later edits never alter past transcripts.

05Choose the discussion by its outcome

These are three distinct workflows, not three names for the same meeting. You can start at any appropriate approach; there is no mandatory brainstorm-then-evaluate-then-scope sequence.

Brainstorm

Use when

The solution space is open. You want practical and bolder alternatives, idea development or priorities for the next investigation.

Do not use when

You need approval, committed spending or a delivery plan for an already selected idea.

Brainstorm with dba-team: how could we improve database reliability? Develop alternatives and prioritize the next experiments.

Evaluate

Use when

A concrete proposal needs a support, oppose or conditional judgment, with challenged trade-offs and explicit conditions or escalation.

Do not use when

Your main need is an open-ended idea backlog or the boundaries and phases of delivery.

Have dba-team evaluate whether we should consolidate database platforms this year, considering cost, resilience and migration risk.

Scope

Use when

A specific idea has a move-forward basis and needs success measures, boundaries, phases, dependencies and blocker mitigations.

Do not use when

You only have a ranking, direction is unclear, or you need a proceed decision, detailed tickets or implementation.

We want to move forward with a reliability pilot. Have dba-team scope it, including boundaries, phases, blockers and mitigations.

Understand scope readiness

A combined scope plan covers outcomes, success measures, in/out boundaries, deliverables, assumptions, constraints, phases, exit criteria, proposed owners/timing, dependencies, risks, blockers, dissent, open questions and next steps. Unknown capacity, owners and dates stay unknown.

blocked

An open blocker gates start. The discussion can be complete even though the plan cannot start.

conditional

Open blockers gate later phases. State what can proceed and what must wait.

ready_for_review

No open blockers gate the plan. This is readiness for review, not implementation approval.

Each blocker has an impact, proposed mitigation, proposed owner, blocking gate, clearance criterion, evidence and open/resolved status. A mitigation proposal is not clearance evidence. If no feasible mitigation is known, say so, retain the blocker and propose investigation or escalation.

06Run with an explicit account and team

  1. Select the account and name the team. If no team is named, the assistant asks even if only one exists. Conflicts between the request and saved question must be resolved.
  2. Provide the question and context. State the intended workflow, constraints, evidence and unknowns. Free text can be saved as a fresh question by the assistant.
  3. Confirm the room. Omit attendees for all participants, or list only a participant subset from the definition. The leader always attends and is not in that list.
  4. Review limitations. Thin profiles, missing expertise and authority gaps must be disclosed. Use person-profile or define-team for approved corrections.
  5. Let the workflow complete and validate. All expected contributions are recorded verbatim. The transcript check gates completed outputs; it checks structure, not the quality or truth of arguments.

No silent room changes. Advisors must already belong to the team. Retired guests: input or --guest overrides cannot bypass membership; convert through an approved team update and fresh input. Participant, advisor and synthetic seats use the orchestrator session model, with no seat-specific model selection.

Structured question: evaluate

Save under the selected team's questions/. New question account metadata and its physical account/team path must agree.

account: example-company
team: dba-team

description: Should we consolidate this year?
background: |
  Describe constraints, evidence and alternatives.

# Optional participant subset; omit for all participants:
# attendees: [sam, jo]
Structured question: brainstorm 
account: example-company
team: dba-team
workflow: brainstorm
topic: Database reliability ideas
description: What could improve reliability with our current capacity?
background: |
  Describe known constraints and evidence; unknowns remain unknown.
priority_criteria: [impact, feasibility, evidence/confidence]

Criteria are framed before generation. Defaults are impact, feasibility and evidence/confidence.

Structured question: scope 
account: example-company
team: dba-team
workflow: scope
topic: Reliability pilot scope
description: Define a bounded pilot and high-level delivery approach.
background: |
  Describe the chosen idea and known evidence.
move_forward_basis: I want this pilot to move forward into scoping.
planning_constraints: |
  Confirm capacity, access approvals and funding before starting.

Alternatively use source_evaluation: teams/dba-team/decisions/<run>-transcript.json instead of move_forward_basis. The source must be inside the selected account; it can come from another team without changing this run's planning room.

A linked completed SUPPORT evaluation qualifies. CONDITIONAL needs conditions explicitly addressed in framing. OPPOSE or incomplete results require clarification or explicit direction to proceed despite them. A recommendation to escalate is not approval by that authority; keep the dependency in the plan.

07Read results and close the learning loop

Read the memo or plan alongside the verbatim discussion. Look for evidence gaps, dissent, assumptions, conditions and escalation, not just the final conclusion.

Complete versus interrupted discussions

Completed runs pass the transcript check before publishing completed outputs and recording workflow memory. Questions are archived only after output and memory verification. Interrupted runs retain a partial record with the cause, leave input pending and do not publish a completed memo or append workflow memory. A complete scope plan can be archived while still explicitly blocked.

Record a real outcome

Record the outcome of the dba-team consolidation decision: the real team deferred it. Compare the simulated room with the supplied outcome evidence and leave unknown individual positions unknown.

record-outcome compares the saved evaluation with real evidence, including leader and advisor calibration. It preserves the original history. Synthetic perspectives never acquire invented real identities or positions. Brainstorm priorities and scope recommendations are not real approve/deny outcomes for calibration.

Compare a different room

Rerun the same evaluation question with extended-leadership. Preserve the original and compare the two rooms after the new decision is recorded.

Reruns use fresh inputs and output names. Prior memos/transcripts are withheld until the new result is recorded, then compared. Eligible own memory may mention the prior run, so this is not a blind test. Team/run provenance keeps local lessons from being mistaken for universal traits.

08Maintain evidence, profiles and teams

Maintenance is incremental. Refresh the scope you need, reuse known evidence, and preserve supported unchanged content. Refreshing company context does not automatically authorize refreshing existing real-person dossiers or changing team membership.

Refresh company coverage

Refresh Example Company's financial and strategy context for the latest filings and earnings call. Reuse existing evidence, leave unchanged information alone and report material changes and unresolved gaps. Do not refresh person profiles.

Use company-research. Expect added, updated, reused, checked-unchanged and blocked coverage, source reuse, before/after differences and next checks. Unchanged checks update the ledger, not evidence dates.

Refresh a person explicitly

Refresh the existing CTO profile using person-profile. Recheck current role and priorities, preserve unchanged evidence and flag identity or role changes for review. Do not alter teams or past decisions.

Reuse the stable ID and prior sources. A failed lookup preserves the existing dossier and last successful evidence state. A change of occupant requires an explicit identity/history decision.

Ingest evidence or fill gaps

Ingest this annual report into the existing company context, reusing saved evidence. Research the open technical gaps and report anything that remains unresolved.

Company-research handles company coverage; person-profile owns person material. Store authorized originals in account-local source folders and record concise sourced summaries, not speculative filler.

Review the room after changes

Review the available profiles and dba-team definition after the research refresh. Propose any needed membership or authority changes for my approval before applying them.

Use define-team for membership changes. Refreshes never silently replace identities, reset memory or rewrite snapshots. Start a fresh Copilot session after repository upgrades.

Keep evidence separate from simulation memory. Company-research, person-profile and record-outcome own researched dossier changes. Discussion seats append only their own designated memory; the facilitator may collect gaps. Memory is excluded from knowledge indexing and profile evidence checks. More discussion history does not make a profile more grounded.

Reference and compatibility

Normal operation uses prompts. The assistant runs the helpers after explicit account selection; placeholders below are not literal customer values. Helpers create and validate structure, not researched facts.

Account, research and profile helpers 
python3 scripts/accounts.py list
python3 scripts/accounts.py new <account>
python3 scripts/build_index.py <account>
python3 scripts/build_index.py <account> --check
python3 scripts/profiles.py new <account> <profile-id> --person "Alex Example" --title "Database Lead" --area technical
python3 scripts/profiles.py new <account> <profile-id> --generic --title "Database Lead" --area technical
python3 scripts/profiles.py check <account> <profile-id>

--area can repeat. Profile new refuses existing IDs. Use person-profile for reviewed refreshes; unused leadership template seats are filled in place, not created again.

Team and transcript helpers 
python3 scripts/teams.py profiles <account>
python3 scripts/teams.py list <account>
python3 scripts/teams.py new <account> <team> --leader leader --participant cfo --purpose "Strategic decisions"
python3 scripts/teams.py check <account> <team>
python3 scripts/teams.py resolve <account> <team> --input-team <team>
python3 scripts/render_transcript.py <transcript-path> --check
python3 scripts/render_transcript.py <transcript-path>

--participant, --advisor and --facilitation can repeat when creating a team. Team resolve/check accept repeatable --attendee. Transcript checks validate frozen-room participation and workflow structure, not approval, evidence truth or mitigation effectiveness.

Existing workspaces, old memory and the convene rename

After repository updates, exit old sessions and start a fresh Copilot session. Reloading skills alone does not reload instructions or hooks. Define desired named teams explicitly; customer files already on disk are left untouched.

The former convene decision workflow is now evaluate. Legacy workflow: convene maps to evaluate; existing questions without a workflow remain decision questions. Use workflow: evaluate for new decision input. Historical outputs are not renamed or rewritten.

Legacy team-local person memories are not read as a fallback. After account selection, explicitly import the history for each profile/team you want to retain:

python3 scripts/profiles.py migrate-memory <account> <profile-id> --team <team>

The import appends original content with team provenance to profile memory, preserves source files and existing entries, skips identical repeat imports and stops on changed sources for manual reconciliation. Never import synthetic archetypes into people profiles.

Rendering requires named-team identity and snapshotted display names; outcome recording also requires the saved room/definition snapshot. Incomplete historical records are rejected, not silently assigned today's team.
