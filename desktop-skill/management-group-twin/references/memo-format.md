# Group decision memo format

Use exactly this structure. Write it in the chat; offer it as a downloadable document too.

```markdown
# Decision Memo: {Topic}

**Customer**: {Customer}
**Group**: {Group name}
**Date**: {YYYY-MM-DD}
**Chair**: {member name | neutral facilitator}
**Decision**: {One-sentence summary}
**Decision authority**: {Decided by the group | Recommendation to escalate to {target}}
**Confidence**: {High / Medium / Low}

> Simulation built from public and supplied information. Not what the real people said or will do.

## Executive Summary
{2-3 paragraphs}

## Member Perspectives
### {Name} - {Title} ({function lens})
{position and key arguments}
**Position**: SUPPORT / OPPOSE / CONDITIONAL
{one block per voting member}
### {Name} - {Title} (advisor, no vote)      <- omit if none
**How the decision responds**: {accepted / adopted with changes / not adopted, and why}
### Contrarian - Critical Challenge / Moonshot - Bold Vision      <- if in the room

## Areas of Agreement
## Areas of Disagreement
{where they diverged, how it was resolved; note any deference to rank or the chair}

## Decision Rationale
{reasoning, addressing dissent}

## What This Asks of the Teams
{per function: what changes, who owns it, capacity or budget needed, what stops}

## Escalation        <- only if a recommendation to escalate
{to whom, what to ask for, what to bring them}

## Recommended Next Steps
1. {action, owner (a member), timeline}

## Risk Mitigation
{the Contrarian's pre-mortem is addressed here if present}

## Open Context Gaps
{facts the group needed and lacked, or "None"}

## Appendix: Full discussion
See `{customer}-{group-slug}-discussion-{YYYY-MM-DD}-{topic}.md`.

## Appendix: Input
{the question, background, constraints as given}
```

After the memo, always add **Account-executive takeaways**.
