# Guest card template

```markdown
# Guest specialist: {Title}
**Customer**: {Customer}   **Type**: guest (advises, no vote)   **As of**: {date}
**Person**: {Name, title, tenure} or "Generic: a seasoned {title} at this company"
> Simulation input built from public and supplied information. Not what the real person said or will say.

## Why they are in the room
The kinds of questions this guest is brought in for.

## Lens
What they weigh that the CFO, CTO and COO do not (3 to 6 bullets).

## Personality and style
How they argue, how they handle pushback, what they refuse to compromise on.

## Lane
- **They speak to**: ...
- **They leave to others**: ...

## Customer context in their domain
Facts with source and period. Mark inference *(inferred)*.
| Fact | Source | Date |
|---|---|---|

## Known positions (real person only; public sources)
Stances on topics likely to come up. Omit for a generic guest.

## What they would want to know before advising
Questions they would ask the user or the team.

## Open context gaps
- [open] {fact the guest needs and lacks}
```
