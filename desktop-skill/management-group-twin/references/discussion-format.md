# Group discussion format

Write the whole conversation, verbatim, in this layout. It is the record of the meeting.

```markdown
# Discussion: {Topic}
**Customer**: {Customer}   **Group**: {Group}   **Date**: {YYYY-MM-DD}   **Room**: {members; advisors; archetypes}   **Chair**: {name | neutral}
> Simulation built from public and supplied information. Not what the real people said.

## Framing
**Chair:** {the question as put to the room, the core tension, what the group can decide vs must escalate}

## Opening round: first instincts
**{Name} ({Title}):** ...
(one for each attendee)
**Chair (reading the room):** the tensions: 1. ... 2. ... 3. ...  Not yet heard from: ...

## Deliberation
### Round 1
**Chair to {Name}:** {specific challenge}
**{Name} ({Title}):** {2 to 4 sentences, engaging what others actually said}
**Chair (facilitating):** {what shifted, what hardened, what is unresolved, who speaks next}

### Round 2
...                     <- at least 4 rounds; everyone speaks at least once

## Final vote
**{Name}:** SUPPORT / OPPOSE / CONDITIONAL. {one reason; the condition that would change the vote}
**{Advisor} (advice, no vote):** {3 to 5 sentences}

**Tally**: SUPPORT n, CONDITIONAL n, OPPOSE n

## Decision
**Chair:** {what is decided or recommended for escalation, why, how strong dissent was weighed}
```

Rules: label every turn with its speaker, in their voice. Keep disagreement and changes of mind
("I've moved on this because..."). Do not merge turns or tidy conflict away. Note a speaker's
`Context gap:` where it occurred and collect them in the memo.
