# Questions only the account executive can answer

Ask five at a time. "Don't know" is a fine answer; record it as a gap. Mark each answer
first-hand (you were in the room), second-hand (someone told you) or inferred.

## The buying picture
1. Who actually owns the data and analytics decision: the CIO, CDO, CTO, a business line?
2. Who holds the budget, and when is the budget cycle? Is there money this year?
3. Is there an active initiative, RFP, pilot or board mandate we should know about?
4. What has changed recently (new executive, reorganisation, acquisition, outage, cost programme)?

## The people
5. Which of the CEO, CFO, CTO and COO have you met or heard speak? What were they like?
6. Who is your champion? Who is skeptical or hostile, and why?
7. Who influences the CFO and CTO (a trusted lieutenant, a consultancy, a board member)?
8. What does each leader care about right now that is not in the public record?

## The technology and competition
9. What do they run today for data, analytics and AI? Where is Teradata present or absent?
10. Which competitors or incumbents are in the account (cloud providers, lakehouse, warehouse vendors, consultancies)?
11. What have they tried and abandoned, and what did they say about it?
12. What has Teradata done in this account before (wins, losses, support issues, pricing history)?

## The politics and constraints
13. What would make them say no regardless of merit (sunk cost, a sponsor's reputation, a lock-in, security or regulatory limits)?
14. Are there sensitivities to avoid raising?
15. Anything you have been told in confidence that you are **not** cleared to put into Claude? (Don't tell me. Leave it out.)
